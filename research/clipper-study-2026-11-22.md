# TikTok clipper study — Nov 22, 2026

Collected in Nicholas's Chrome (watch/read only — nothing liked, followed, posted or commented).

**Sample:** 974 recent clipper videos (not the streamers' own accounts; 5–90 s; last 60 days) from 11 searches:
Lacy, Kai Cenat, IShowSpeed, Clix, YourRAGE, xQc, Ludwig, GTA 6 streamer reactions.
- Stats for all 974; first frames for 194 winners (100K+ views) + 40 flops (<3K); first-second playback for 22 GTA winners.
- Audio timing (loudest moment, first second, ending) for 128 clips; 16 same-moment groups compared.

## Findings
| | Winners (100K+) | Flops (<3K) |
|---|---|---|
| Hook length (GTA) | ~13 words | ~9 words |
| Hook with 1–2 CAPS words | 63% | 48% |
| All-caps hook | 13% | 10% |
| Emoji in hook | 92% | 81% |
| Caption length | ~6–8 words | ~11 words |
| Loudest moment | ~12 s into ~25 s (53%) | ~15 s into ~35 s |
| Peak in first 3 s | 15% | 18% |
| First loud spike | 0.75 s | 2 s |
| Page followers (median) | ~62K | ~15 |

- Same moment, several pages: the **first poster got the most views in 9 of 16** groups (often 10–40×). 15 h+ late posts almost never won.
- A 1K-follower page got 2.4M with "Kai Cenat devastated finding out you can't free roam after GTA 6 ends"; the same hook in caps on a 19K page got 82K.
- GTA layout (webcam top, gameplay bottom, white box at the seam, text on the whole clip) is the same for winners and flops — not the difference.

## What changed in the clip maker
- `best_part` / `find_moments`: build-up first, loudest moment ~45–55% in, ~22 s, start on someone talking (learner still tests the 2 s "fast" start).
- Hooks: one story sentence, 8–15 words, exactly 1–2 CAPS words, strong reaction verb, keep the striking specific; no emojis on the video (Nicholas's rule).
- Poster: clips under 2 h old get +25 priority, under 4 h +12.
- Learner (LEARN_V 4): len s16/s22, hooklen tiny/story, start fast/build — priors from this study.
