## Hook rules
- Max ~60 chars (aim 35-50), plain text, no emojis on video, no swearing.
- Lead with the streamer name OR the funny game fact; then one concrete noun (ATM, drone, forklift).
- At most ONE caps word, and only when it is the point (FIRST, AGAIN, HOUR). Often zero.
- Ban list: STREAMER, SHOCKED, STUNNED, LOSES IT, FURIOUS, DISGUSTS, raw stream titles, timestamps.
- "Why is X ...?" question hooks: testing (Nov 21 evening), no data yet.
- Stay true to title + transcript; when the transcript is thin, keep the hook modest.
- Captions may carry emojis/hashtags; the video hook box may not.

## Always skip

  revealing outfits, "girl"/"gay" talk that could read badly, strippers/clubs, animal harm,
  drugs-as-the-hook, Rockstar trailers/footage, other clippers' reposts, non-GTA footage
  (e.g. FNCS commentary), mostly-cutscene/radio clips, real-life talk about death,
  duplicate moments (VOD clips from the same stream within ~60s usually overlap).
