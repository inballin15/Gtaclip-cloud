## What doesn't
- Dialogue-quote hooks ("Girl you okay? ... hormones") and vague ones ("can't believe this") = 0 views.
- Duplicate posts of the same clip all got 0 except one. One clip = one post.
- YouTube Shorts get almost nothing so far (unverified channel, upload caps).
- The AI template "STREAMER LOSES IT / is SHOCKED after DISCOVERING..." — too many caps, too long,
  generic. Rewrite always (recent manual posts still used it).

## Hook rules
- Max ~70 chars (aim 35-55), plain text, no emojis on video, no swearing.
- Lead with the streamer name OR the funny game fact; then one concrete noun.
- One or two caps words for the key moment (HOUR, FIRST, CRASH, WORST, MILLION).
- No "STREAMER", no "LOSES IT/SHOCKED" filler, no raw stream titles or chat-spam.
- Stay true to title + transcript; when the transcript is thin, keep the hook modest.
- Captions may carry emojis/hashtags; the video hook box may not.

  revealing-outfit scenes, any "girl" talk that could read sexual, strippers/clubs, animal harm, drugs-as-the-hook, Rockstar trailers/footage,
  other clippers' reposts, mostly-cutscene/radio clips, real-life talk about death,
  duplicate moments of the same clip (VOD clips from the same stream often repeat a moment).
